About Me

As a resident of the Genesis community, I care deeply about maintaining our neighborhood's value, safety, and shared quality of life. I am seeking a seat on the Genesis HOA Board to help ensure our community is managed efficiently, transparently, and with every homeowner's interests in mind.

My background in organizational management, budgeting oversight, and collaborative problem-solving equips me to serve effectively on the board and work productively alongside neighbors and management.

Key Priorities

1. Fiscal Responsibility & Reserve Health

Ensure HOA dues are spent wisely with full auditing transparency while maintaining a healthy reserve fund for long-term repairs and capital improvements.

2. Clear & Consistent Communication

Keep residents well-informed with timely meeting summaries, clear policy updates, and open channels for homeowner feedback.

3. Fair Policy Enforcement & Property Care

Maintain high standards for common area maintenance and ensure community guidelines are applied fairly and reasonably across all homes.
