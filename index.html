About Me

As a resident of the Genesis community, I care deeply about maintaining our neighborhood's value, safety, and shared quality of life. I am seeking a seat on the Genesis HOA Board to help ensure our community is managed efficiently, transparently, and with every homeowner's interests in mind. I want to learn what you find is the most important issues facing our community, so please contact me at thewayllc@gmail.com

My background in organizational management, budgeting oversight, and collaborative problem-solving equips me to serve effectively on the board and work productively alongside neighbors and management.I have been employed at Long Beach City College as a Librarian/Professor for 9 years and deal with vendors, contractors and budgets on a daily basis.

Key Priorities

1. Fiscal Responsibility & Reserve Health

Ensure HOA dues are spent wisely with full auditing transparency while maintaining a healthy reserve fund for long-term repairs and capital improvements. I pledge to never vote for or explore another assessment which was done in 2016, where each unit was assessed around $20,000. Through proper budget management we can maintain and upgrade our community together!

2. Clear & Consistent Communication

Keep residents well-informed with timely meeting summaries, clear policy updates, and open channels for homeowner feedback (email me and I will respond in 24 hours). If elected I will publish a monthly newsletter that includes a summary of all HOA activities.

3. Fair Policy Enforcement & Property Care

Maintain high standards for common area maintenance and ensure community guidelines are applied fairly and reasonably across all homes.
